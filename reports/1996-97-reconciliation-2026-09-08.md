## 1. Fixed league controls

1996/97 was a 20-club Premier League season with 38 league matches per club / 380 league matches total.

Therefore the exact league-wide starting-player total is:

`380 matches × 22 starters = 8,360 starts`

Equivalent club control:

`20 clubs × 38 matches × 11 starters = 8,360 starts`

Any season dataset labelled as league starts must reconcile to **8,360**.

The later checkpoint's **9,108 starts** is therefore impossible as a Premier League start total.

StatBunker `comp_id=9` independently supports the control: its club appearance tables total **418 starts per club** (38 × 11). Examples checked during this reconciliation included Arsenal, Chelsea, Coventry City, Everton and Newcastle United.

### Goal control

The 1996/97 Premier League produced **970 league goals** in 380 matches.

The later checkpoint's **1,133 goals** cannot represent 1996/97 Premier League goals.

### Discipline control

Independent season discipline records report **43 red cards** across the 1996/97 Premier League season. The later checkpoint's **67 send-offs** is therefore not a valid season red-card total.

These three independent control failures are sufficient to reject the later checkpoint as a season-complete authority even before row-level inspection.
