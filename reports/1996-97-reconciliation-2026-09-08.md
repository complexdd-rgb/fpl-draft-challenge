## Hard league controls

1996/97 was a 20-club, 380-match Premier League season.

- 38 matches per club × 11 starters = **418 starts per club**.
- 20 clubs × 418 starts = **8,360 league starts**.
- Official league goals = **970**.

The later 9,108-start checkpoint is therefore invalid regardless of its row count.
